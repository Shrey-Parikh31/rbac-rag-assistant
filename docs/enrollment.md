role: public
title: Course Enrollment
---
Students may enroll in courses through the student portal during the open
enrollment window. The window opens eight weeks before the semester begins and
closes on the first Friday of classes.

Late enrollment requires the instructor's written approval and incurs a fee of
$75. Requests submitted after the second week of classes are not accepted under
any circumstance.

A student may carry a maximum of 18 credits per semester. Exceeding this
requires approval from the academic advisor and a cumulative GPA of at least
3.0.
