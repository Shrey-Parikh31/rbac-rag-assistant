title: Faculty Compensation Bands
---
Assistant Professor: $78,000 to $96,000 depending on discipline and prior
appointment. Associate Professor: $95,000 to $124,000. Full Professor:
$120,000 to $187,000.

Adjunct instructors are compensated per credit hour at $1,450 for the 2026 to
2027 academic year.

These figures are internal and must not be disclosed to students or external
parties.
