Staff must report suspected security incidents to the IT service desk within
one hour of discovery. Do not attempt to remediate a suspected compromise
independently.

Severity one incidents, defined as any event affecting student data or
authentication systems, page the on-call engineer immediately at any hour.

All incidents require a written postmortem within five business days,
regardless of severity or impact.
